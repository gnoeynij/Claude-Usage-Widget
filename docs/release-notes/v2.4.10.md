# v2.4.10 — Opus 5 cost accounting & milestone toast fixes

A fast-follow patch: Claude Opus 5 usage was silently counted as $0, and the
$25k milestone toast could not be closed.

## Fixed

- **Claude Opus 5 usage counted as $0.** The pricing table had no
  `claude-opus-5` entry, and since `claude-opus-4` is not a prefix of
  `claude-opus-5`, every Opus 5 record silently resolved to zero cost. Opus 5
  now prices at its official $5 / $25 per MTok (same tier as Opus 4.8, cache
  6.25 / 10 / 0.5), with fast mode at $10 / $50 — verified against the
  official model table on 2026-07-28. Daily history for days whose JSONL
  still exists is corrected on the next sync; lifetime totals accrued while
  the bug was live are not retroactively adjusted.
- **The $25,000 milestone toast couldn't be closed.** The milestone window's
  label was missing from the capability manifest, so the "I'm proud" button
  and ESC were both silently rejected by the IPC allowlist — and the window
  is always-on-top, leaving no way to dismiss it short of restarting the
  widget.
- **Milestone toast showed a white frame.** The window painted forced-light
  Mica across its whole surface, which leaked around the card as a white
  border (stark against a dark-mode card). The toast is now a pure floating
  glass card — no OS material, the desktop shows through around it.

## Notes

- All costs are *estimates* derived from local JSONL × official pricing
  (subscribers are not billed per-token).
- macOS: builds ship and auto-update as usual, but this release's paths
  remain field-unverified on real hardware.
